Can you see with an implant's eyes?

People who are blind may one day see again through an implant in the visual cortex. It would not show a picture, only a few hundred dots of light. This short test asks how well people can use those dots, and which way of drawing them works best.

  • About 10 minutes, in two parts: reading letters, then spotting obstacles.
  • Each part starts with 3 practice rounds that show you the answer.
  • Many pictures are hard on purpose. Guess when unsure; there is no penalty.
Your answers are not saved. They stay in this browser tab and disappear when you close it. This is an informal pilot, not a research study, and no results are published.

Works best on a laptop or desktop, in a dim room. Keyboard shortcuts are available.
